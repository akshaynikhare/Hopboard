Getting the clipboard to move between two machines is more fiddly than it should be, and most of the reasons are not obvious. These pages fix the built-in features first, and say plainly where there is no fix.

  • Installing RealtimeClipboard on any device

    Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iPhone and the command line — what to install on each, and which of them can watch the clipboard in the background. Two of them cannot, and the reason is not this app.

  • Clipboard sync not working?

    Why “Sync across your devices” is greyed out on Windows 11 — the cause almost every guide skips — plus fixes for Samsung, Android, Mac and iPhone, and what your employer can block.
